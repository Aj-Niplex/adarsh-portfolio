## Fit
- One owner or a small allow-list.
- Chat-only control.
- A small free Python host.

## Not a fit
- Teams that need accounts, roles and audit logs.
- Anyone who needs strong isolation. The sandbox is not a container.
- Heavy workloads.

## Compared with others
See the comparisons file in 12-project. It makes no claim of being better.
